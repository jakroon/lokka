Shipping & delivery

From Turgutreis to your door.

Stock items ship in 1–2 working days, printed items 2–5 working days after proof approval. Free standard shipping over TRY 1,500.

Production times

When will it be ready?

Times are in working days and start after payment and, for personalised items, after proof approval. For rush jobs, message us on WhatsApp and we’ll check our schedule.

Tracking

When your order ships, the tracking number is sent by SMS and email. You can check its progress on the Order Tracking page with your order number.

Damaged or missing items

If the parcel looks damaged, ask the courier to file a report before you accept it. For missing or wrong items, message us on WhatsApp with photos within 3 days of delivery and we will reship.

International shipping

For international delivery, message us on WhatsApp with the products, quantity and country and we’ll quote the cost and timing.
