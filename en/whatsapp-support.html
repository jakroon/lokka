All questions
Can I order a single printed item?

Yes. We produce from a single piece with DTF and vinyl. Screen printing starts at 30 pieces and embroidery at 10.

What should my artwork file look like?

For best results send vector (SVG, PDF, AI) or a transparent PNG at 300 DPI or more. 3000 pixels wide at print size is enough for most jobs.

When will my order ship?

Unprinted stock items ship in 1–2 working days; printed and personalised items ship 2–5 working days after proof approval.

What are the bulk discounts?

10% at 20 pieces, 15% at 50, 20% at 100 and 25% from 250. Different sizes and colours can be combined in one order.
